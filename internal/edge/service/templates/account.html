Delete this account

Deleting it removes the account, its device tokens and its sessions at this edge, and closes its open connections. Every server below is told, now or when it next connects, and removes this identity and its edge devices. No server removes a member, changes a role or deletes data because of it.

What stays: an SSH key or tailnet identity you use on a server keeps working until an admin removes it there, and an invitation to your login or email stays until that server's admin revokes it. Signing in again later creates a new account.
